About Whyzy

Hi, I’m 一只问号吖 (pronounced Yi-Zhi-Wen-Hao-Ya), and my English handle is Whyzy. I used to go by Yzwh_Y, and sometimes my IME auto-corrects my Chinese name to "一只问号丫" on certain platforms.

Let me share the story behind my English name. Initially, Yzwh_Y came from the Pinyin initials of my Chinese name. Interestingly, the "wh_Y" part naturally spells out "why", which fits the whole "question mark" vibe perfectly. However, it was quite a mouthful to pronounce. After a late-night brainstorming session with Claude, I finally settled on Whyzy (pronounced "Why-zee"). Essentially, I just swapped the first two letters of YzwhY and moved the "why" to the front—and suddenly, it became way more readable!

I have a wide range of hobbies, though I’m admittedly a jack-of-all-trades and master of none—most of the time, I just scratch the surface. Case in point: this very paragraph was actually translated by Gemini! Still, I hope to keep learning through all my future tinkering.

—— Whyzy
